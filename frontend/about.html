ABOUT MoviezDB

A fast, free way to discover movies and TV — trailers, ratings, and where to watch.

What it is

MoviezDB is a movie & TV discovery app. Browse what's trending, popular, and top-rated, search across films, shows and people, dig into rich detail pages, and build your own watchlist and favourites — all in your language, in light or dark mode.

It's a personal project, built to be quick and clutter-free. No accounts required to browse; your watchlist lives right in your browser.

Features

Trending hero — an auto-rotating spotlight of what's hot today.
Discover — filter by genre, year, rating and sort.
Trailers — play official YouTube trailers in one tap.
Where to watch — legal streaming links via JustWatch.
Watchlist & favourites — saved locally on your device.
12 languages — including full right-to-left Arabic.

How you can watch

MoviezDB does not host or stream films. It plays official trailers from YouTube and points you to legitimate streaming services through JustWatch's "where to watch" data. Every link takes you to a legal, licensed source.
